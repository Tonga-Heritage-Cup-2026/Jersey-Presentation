What to Expect

A warm afternoon together — here's a taste of what's ahead

A warm welcome from our Faifekau
Opening prayer, the Tongan National Anthem sung together, and a blessing over the jerseys before they meet our players.
The moment we've all been waiting for
Team by team, from our smallest U7s to our U12s, every one of our 78 boys will be called by name and family and receive their heritage jersey from a loved one their family has chosen.
Words to inspire our boys
A guest speaker will share a few words with our players ahead of the tournament.
Sipi tau for the families
Our teams will perform the sipi tau together — a chance for parents, grandparents and elders to see the boys stand tall as one squad.
Photo time all around the hall
Photo opportunities roam through the whole hall — not one big line on stage. Move about with your child, gather with family and friends, and create the heritage group photos we've waited for.
A song and a blessing to send us home
We'll close together with Himi 391 — 'Oku ai ha ki'i fonua — and a final blessing from our Faifekau. Doors close at 7:00pm.

Tongan National Anthem

Ko e fasi ʻo e tuʻi ʻo e ʻOtu Tonga — sung together after the opening prayer

ʻE ʻOtua Māfimafi,
Ko ho mau ʻEiki koe,
Ko Koe ko e falalaʻanga,
Mo e ʻofa ki Tonga:

ʻAfio hifo ʻemau lotu
ʻA ia ʻoku mau fai ni,
Mo ke tali ho mau loto
ʻO maluʻi ʻa Tupou.

Oh almighty God above,
Thou art our lord and sure defense,
As your people, we trust thee
And our Tonga thou dost love.

Hear our prayer for thou unseen,
We know that thou hath blessed our land,
Grant our earnest supplication,
God save Tupou, our king.

Himi 391

'Oku ai ha ki'i fonua — sung together as one heritage before the final blessing

'Oku ai ha ki'i fonua
'Oku tu'u 'i 'Oseni.
Na'e 'ikai ke ma'u 'Otua,
Na'e masiva he lelei.
Haleluia! Kuo monū'ia eni.

Tama Tonga, tu'u 'o ngāue,
Ho koloa ke fakamonū.
Lotu ki he 'Eiki ma'u pē,
Ke ne poupou ki he lotu
'O malu'i,
'O malu'i 'a Tupou.

There is a small Island
that stands in the Ocean.
It did not have God,
They were poor in goodness.
Hallelujah! They are now blessed.

Special Touches

Our Heritage Cultural Leader

We are honoured to name John Hopoate as our Heritage Cultural Leader for the Tonga Heritage Cup 2026, recognising his role supporting our teams and families behind the scenes.

Honoured Presenters

Each player's jersey will be presented by someone their family has chosen. We ask families to first consider a grandparent to do the honours — so our elders can be the ones to place the heritage jersey on their moko'puna.

Where a grandparent isn't able to be there, please choose a special family member or a community role model your child looks up to.

Families: please let your team manager know the name of the person presenting your child's jersey when you RSVP, so we can call them up correctly on the night.

What Makes This Evening Ours

  • Every player called individually — by name and family
  • Photo opportunities all around the hall — families welcome to gather with each other and create the group photos that will hang on our walls for years to come
  • Wall of Names displaying every one of our 78 players
