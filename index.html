A Word to Our Families

This is a moment we will remember forever. For the very first time, our TFW9s players will wear the red of Tonga at TONGA HERITAGE — stepping out in the colours of the Kingdom, carrying the pride of our elders and the hopes of every family that came before them.

Come with your whole family — young and old, near and far. Wear your ta'ovala. Bring your voice. This is our TONGA HERITAGE, and it begins with this night.

Our Faifekau will open the presentation and bless the jerseys. Honoured grandparents, family and role models will hand them to each player. Every family named will be honoured aloud.
